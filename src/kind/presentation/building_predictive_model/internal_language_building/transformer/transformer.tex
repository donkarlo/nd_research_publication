\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Predictive brain - Transformers as predictors for Kalman filter}
Because different compositions of language segments form different meanings, LSTMs (and consequently RNNs) cannot capture all local dependencies. A multi-head-attention predictor is therefore needed; Transformers provide this mechanism \cite{vaswani-2017-attention-is-all-you-need}.

Its application here is to replace the constant-speed predictor.

\item Noise as uncertainty in the Transformer

\end{itemize}
