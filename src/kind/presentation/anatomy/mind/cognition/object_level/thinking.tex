\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Thinking}
    Changing the structure of a model, such as the architecture of layers in a neural network.
    
    In this research, thinking can be seen as switching between Transformers or dynamic Bayesian networks as the forecaster.
    
    Thinking is composed of the following parts, which this research also addresses:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Planning: produces an action tree from a goal (sensorimotor control is part of planning)
        \item Decision making: choosing the appropriate super-state according to the observation and the motor decision made by the flight controller
        \item Concept formation: The whole process of building the language
    \end{itemize}

\end{frame}
