\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Attention as a Cognitive process}
    When the brain tries to follow a goal, such as generating a sensorimotor control signal to follow a trajectory, the ventral attentional network becomes active\cite{scalf-2014-trial-history-effects-in-the-ventral-attentional-network}
    
    In this work, attention is considered in the continuous relationship among planning, perception, and control while fulfilling goals such as following a trajectory.
\end{frame}
