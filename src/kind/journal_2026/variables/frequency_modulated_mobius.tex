         & Bound: $\omega \in (0, 1]$. It controls the sharpness, skewness, and temporal deformation of the waveform. When $\omega$ is close to zero, the waveform becomes more spike-like; when $\omega$ is close to one, the waveform becomes more similar to a sinusoidal wave. \\
