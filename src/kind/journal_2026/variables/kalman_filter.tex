Posterior state estimate & $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t$ & Final state estimate at time $t$ after the KF correction. \\
Posterior covariance & $\mathbf{P}_t$ & Uncertainty of the final state estimate after the KF correction. \\
Identity matrix & $\mathbf{I}$ & Identity matrix used in the posterior covariance update. \\
