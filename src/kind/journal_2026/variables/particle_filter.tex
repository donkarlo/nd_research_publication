\textbf{Concept} & \textbf{Variable name} & \textbf{Description} \\

Previous state estimate & $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t-1}$ & Final estimate from the preceding time step and recurrent input to the PF. \\
Representation level & $\ell$ & Level of the hierarchical representation traversed by the PF. \\
Class at level $\ell$ & $C_k^{(\ell)}$ & The $k$th learned class at representation level $\ell$. \\
Class probability & $\pi_{t,k}^{(\ell)}$ & PF probability assigned to class $C_k^{(\ell)}$ at time $t$. \\
Vowel class & $C_k^{(v)}$ & Learned class of first derivatives of AP-FMM parameters, interpreted as velocity. \\
Velocity observation & $\mathbf{v}_{k,j}$ & The $j$th learned velocity observation belonging to vowel class $k$. \\
Vowel empirical distribution & $\mu_k^{(v)}$ & Empirical probability distribution of all velocity observations in vowel class $k$. \\
PF velocity prediction & $p_{\mathrm{PF}}(\mathbf{v}_t)$ & Class-probability-weighted mixture of vowel empirical distributions. \\
Motion model & $g(\cdot)$ & Propagates the previous estimate using a sampled/class-conditioned velocity. \\
PF predicted state & $\mathbf{x}_t^{(k,j)}$ & State obtained by propagating $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t-1}$ with velocity observation $\mathbf{v}_{k,j}$. \\
PF predictive mean & $\boldsymbol{\mu}_t^{\mathrm{PF}}$ & Weighted mean of the PF predicted-state particles. \\
PF predictive covariance & $\mathbf{P}_t^{\mathrm{PF}}$ & Weighted covariance of the PF predicted-state particles. \\
