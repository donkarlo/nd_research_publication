         \hline

         \textbf{Concept} & \textbf{Variable name} & \textbf{Description} \\

         \hline

         Forecasting model
         & $f_{\theta}$
         & Gaussian Transformer that maps an input time-series window to the parameters of future Gaussian distributions. \\

         Model parameters
         & $\theta$
         & Trainable parameters of the forecasting model. \\

         Input horizon
         & $T_{\mathrm{in}}$
         & Number of time steps contained in the input window. \\

         Output horizon
         & $T_{\mathrm{out}}$
         & Number of future time steps predicted by the model. \\

         Number of input features
         & $F_{\mathrm{in}}$
         & Number of features contained in each input time step. \\

         Number of output features
         & $F_{\mathrm{out}}$
         & Number of features predicted at each future time step. \\

         Predicted Gaussian parameters
         & $\hat{Y}_{\mathrm{params}}$
         & Model output containing the predicted mean and log-variance. \\

         Predicted mean
         & $\mu$
         & Expected future value predicted by the model. \\

         Predicted log-variance
         & $\log \sigma^2$
         & Logarithm of the predicted variance. Predicting log-variance allows the variance to be recovered as a positive value. \\

         Predicted variance
         & $\sigma^2$
         & Predictive uncertainty, obtained as $\sigma^2=\exp(\log\sigma^2)$. \\

         Predicted standard deviation
         & $\sigma$
         & Square root of the predicted variance and used to construct the prediction interval. \\

         Target value
         & $y_{b,t,j}$
         & Observed target value for batch item $b$, output time step $t$, and output feature $j$. \\

         Batch index
         & $b$
         & Index of a sample within the batch. \\

         Output time-step index
         & $t$
         & Index of a time step within the prediction horizon. \\

         Output feature index
         & $j$
         & Index of a predicted output feature. \\

         Batch size
         & $B$
         & Number of samples processed simultaneously during training. \\

         Gaussian distribution
         & $\mathcal{N}$
         & Normal distribution used to represent the predictive distribution of each future value. \\

         Loss function
         & $\mathcal{L}$
         & Gaussian negative log-likelihood used to train both the predicted mean and predictive uncertainty. \\

         \hline

      \end{tabular}

      \caption{Variables used in the Gaussian Transformer probabilistic forecasting formulation.}

      \label{tab:gaussian-transformer-variables}
