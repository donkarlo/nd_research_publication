         Spike sequence at a representation level
         & $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$
         & Ordered sequence of spike events generated by state neurons belonging to representation level $\ell$. \\

         Sequence length
         & $N_{\ell}$
         & Number of spike events contained in $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$. \\

         Sequence-position index
         & $n$
         & Position of a spike event within $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$, where $n\in\{1,\ldots,N_{\ell}\}$. \\

         Candidate subsequence
         & $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}[a:b]$
         & Variable-length candidate subsequence of $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$ starting at index $a$ and ending at index $b$. \\

         Subsequence start index
         & $a$
         & Starting position of a candidate subsequence. \\

         Subsequence end index
         & $b$
         & Ending position of a candidate subsequence. \\

         Motif index
         & $k$
         & Identifier of a discovered motif at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Discovered motif
         & $\mathbf{m}_{k,\ell}$
         & The $k$th recurrent variable-length spike pattern discovered independently at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Motif-distance function
         & $\Delta_{\ell}(\cdot,\cdot)$
         & Distance measure used to compare two subsequences at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Motif acceptance threshold
         & $\varepsilon_{\ell}$
         & Maximum accepted distance for considering two subsequences occurrences of the same motif at representation level $\ell$. \\
