         \hline
         \textbf{Concept} & \textbf{Variable name} & \textbf{Description} \\
         \hline

         Spike sequence at a representation level
         & $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$
         & Ordered sequence of spike events generated by state neurons belonging to representation level $\ell$, where $\ell \in L$ is defined in the state-neuron notation. \\

         Motif-sequence length
         & $N_{\ell}^{\mathrm{mot}}$
         & Number of spike events contained in $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$ for motif discovery at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Subsequence start index
         & $u$
         & Starting position of a variable-length candidate subsequence. \\

         Subsequence end index
         & $v$
         & Ending position of a variable-length candidate subsequence, with $1 \leq u < v \leq N_{\ell}^{\mathrm{mot}}$. \\

         Candidate subsequence
         & $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}[u:v]$
         & Variable-length candidate subsequence of $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}$ extending from index $u$ to index $v$. \\

         Motif GRU encoder
         & $\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{mot}}_{\eta_{\ell}}$
         & GRU encoder trained at representation level $\ell$ to map a variable-length candidate subsequence to a fixed-dimensional latent representation. \\

         Motif GRU decoder
         & $\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{mot}}_{\xi_{\ell}}$
         & Decoder trained to reconstruct a candidate subsequence from its motif latent representation. \\

         Motif-encoder parameters
         & $\eta_{\ell}$
         & Trainable parameters of the motif GRU encoder at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Motif-decoder parameters
         & $\xi_{\ell}$
         & Trainable parameters of the motif decoder at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Candidate latent representation
         & $\mathbf{g}_{\ell,u:v}$
         & Fixed-dimensional latent representation of candidate subsequence $\mathbf{s}_{\ell}[u:v]$, defined by $\mathbf{g}_{\ell,u:v}=\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{mot}}_{\eta_{\ell}}(\mathbf{s}_{\ell}[u:v])$. \\

         Motif index
         & $\kappa$
         & Identifier of a discovered motif at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Latent motif prototype
         & $\mathbf{c}_{\kappa,\ell}$
         & Representative latent vector of the $\kappa$th recurrent pattern at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Discovered motif
         & $\mathbf{m}_{\kappa,\ell}$
         & Representative variable-length spike subsequence associated with latent motif prototype $\mathbf{c}_{\kappa,\ell}$. \\

         Motif-distance function
         & $\Delta^{\mathrm{mot}}_{\ell}(\cdot,\cdot)$
         & Distance measure used to compare latent candidate representations with latent motif prototypes at representation level $\ell$. \\

         Motif acceptance threshold
         & $\varepsilon^{\mathrm{mot}}_{\ell}$
         & Maximum latent-space distance for assigning a candidate subsequence to a motif at representation level $\ell$. \\

         \hline
      \end{tabular}
