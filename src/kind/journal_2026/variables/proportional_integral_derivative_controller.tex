         \hline
         \textbf{Concept} & \textbf{Variable name} & \textbf{Description} \\
         \hline

         Velocity setpoint
         & $\mathbf{v}^{\mathrm{sp}}_k$
         & Desired velocity received from the outer proportional position-control loop at time step $k$. \\

         Current velocity
         & $\mathbf{v}_k$
         & Estimated or measured vehicle velocity at time step $k$. \\

         Velocity tracking error
         & $\mathbf{e}_{v,k}$
         & Difference between desired and current velocity, $\mathbf{e}_{v,k}=\mathbf{v}^{\mathrm{sp}}_k-\mathbf{v}_k$. \\

         Velocity-loop proportional gain
         & $K_{\mathrm{P},v}$
         & Proportional gain applied to the current velocity tracking error. \\

         Velocity-loop integral gain
         & $K_{\mathrm{I},v}$
         & Integral gain applied to the accumulated velocity tracking error. \\

         Velocity-loop derivative gain
         & $K_{\mathrm{D},v}$
         & Derivative gain applied to the change of velocity tracking error. \\

         Sampling interval
         & $\Delta t$
         & Time interval between consecutive control updates. \\

         Summation index
         & $j$
         & Index used to accumulate past velocity tracking errors in the integral term. \\

         Previous velocity tracking error
         & $\mathbf{e}_{v,k-1}$
         & Velocity tracking error from the previous control time step, used in the derivative term. \\

         Low-level control command
         & $\mathbf{c}_k$
         & Command generated by the inner PID velocity-control loop at time step $k$. \\

         Time-step index
         & $k$
         & Discrete control time-step index. \\

         \hline
      \end{tabular}
