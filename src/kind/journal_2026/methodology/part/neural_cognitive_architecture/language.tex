   The role of language here is to provide replaceable components that when they are replaced, then we can assess they meaningfulness as measurement for anomaly detection. In other words they tighten the discrepancy detection conditions.

   You may consider each utterance of a language as a variable length sequence (and not a time series, because language components bear no time indexes unless they are mentioned independently as members of the sequence) of replaceable components which by themselves are formed of other replaceable components. The function of a linguistic system is to assign a probability to each utterance sequence based on the context in which it occurs.

   \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Syntagmatic axis:} language elements combine in a linear sequence, for example words forming a sentence.
      \item \textbf{Paradigmatic axis:} language elements can replace each other in the same position, changing the meaning or value of the expression.
   \end{itemize}

   Saussure argues that linguistic signs acquire their value through their differences and relations with other signs within the language system \cite{saussure-1966-course-in-general-linguistics}. Lévi-Strauss extends this structural principle to culture and myth, where meaning is often organized through relational structures and binary oppositions \cite{levi-strauss-1955-the-structural-study-of-myth}.

   The method we propose in this paper sees anomaly and in a higher level novelty detection as a part of stauss opinion by which we consider the dichotomies such as  anomaly vs. normality or novelty/familiarity  or outlier/inlier as sign of agent.

   If the agent can say "I am aware" such that I is the opposite of "other" and awareness is the opposite unawareness. Then we can say this agent is aware of it's awareness or in other words, it is a self aware agent.

   So the main objective of this research is to make the agent to make a distinction between him and others(the self part of self-awareness) and between novel and familiarity(awarness part of the self awareness).

   In other words The proposed system models a novelty-based component of awareness, particularly the conscious detection of violations of learned global regularities.


   The proposed architecture therefore separates the self-awareness problem into two explicit distinctions:
   \[
   C_{\mathrm{self}} \neq C_{\mathrm{other}}
   \]
   and
   \[
   C_{\mathrm{aware}} \neq C_{\mathrm{unaware}}.
   \]

   The proposition ``I am aware'' can then be interpreted as the combination of a self-representation and a representation of the agent's own awareness state:
   \[
   \text{``I am aware''}
   =
   \text{self-representation}
   +
   \text{representation of awareness}.
   \]

   From a structural perspective, the process can be summarized as
   \[
   \text{sensory data}
   \rightarrow
   \text{distinctions}
   \rightarrow
   \text{categories}
   \rightarrow
   \text{signs}
   \rightarrow
   \text{language}.
   \]
