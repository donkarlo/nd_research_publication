   The motivation for this research is the absence of a neurally and biologically inspired cognitive framework that can connect low-level adaptive mechanisms to progressively richer forms of cognition through linguistically enhanced self-awareness, thereby enhancing agents' autonomy and potentially extending their lifespan by supporting more balanced homeostatic and allostatic regulation.


   \section{A short list of existing frameworks and their deficiencies}
   for a more detailed review please see \seeherefooter{/nd_robotic_ai_learning_project/src/from_phd_review/robotics/from-awareness-to-consciousness.tex}
   
   
      \subsection{ACT-R}
      ACT-R is a symbolic cognitive architecture that models human cognition through interacting memory, perception, and production-rule mechanisms \cite{anderson-1996-act-r}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      It is not primarily designed as a neurally grounded architecture and does not explicitly integrate linguistically enhanced self-awareness with homeostatic or allostatic regulation. It therefore lacks an explicit mechanism linking self-modeling and language to autonomous physiological-style regulation and lifespan-oriented adaptation.

      \subsection{Soar}
      Soar is a general cognitive architecture centered on problem solving, procedural knowledge, learning, and goal-directed decision making \cite{lehman-1996-soar-architecture}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      Its emphasis is on symbolic cognition and task-level autonomy rather than neural or biological regulation, and it does not explicitly connect self-awareness, language, homeostasis, and allostasis within a unified adaptive loop.

      \subsection{CLARION}
      CLARION combines explicit symbolic knowledge with implicit subsymbolic representations and provides a hybrid account of reasoning, learning, and action \cite{sun-2006-clarion-framework}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      Although its dual-representation structure is closer to biologically plausible cognition than purely symbolic systems, it does not explicitly model linguistically enhanced self-awareness or connect metacognitive processes to homeostatic and allostatic regulation.

      \subsection{LIDA}
      LIDA is a biologically inspired cognitive architecture organized around repeated cognitive cycles integrating perception, attention, memory, action selection, and learning \cite{franklin-2013-lida-theory}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      Its biological inspiration and attention mechanisms are relevant to the present goal, but it does not provide an explicit framework in which language strengthens self-awareness and in turn regulates homeostatic and allostatic processes to enhance long-term autonomy.

      \subsection{OpenCog}
      OpenCog is an integrative framework for artificial general intelligence that combines knowledge representation, probabilistic reasoning, learning, and multiple interacting cognitive processes \cite{goertzel-2014-opencog-framework-artificial-general-intelligence}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      It provides rich symbolic and subsymbolic cognitive mechanisms, including language-oriented reasoning, but it is not centered on neural and biological regulation and does not explicitly link linguistic self-awareness to homeostasis, allostasis, or agent lifespan.

      \subsection{SPAUN}
      SPAUN is a large-scale spiking-neural model that integrates perception, working memory, reasoning, and motor behavior in a single biologically inspired system \cite{eliasmith-2012-a-large-scale-model-of-the-functioning-brain}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      It provides strong neural grounding, but its focus is on reproducing a range of cognitive tasks rather than self-awareness, metacognitive regulation, or a language-mediated mechanism for maintaining homeostatic and allostatic balance.

      \subsection{NARS}
      NARS is a reasoning architecture designed to operate under insufficient knowledge and limited computational resources, continuously revising beliefs and selecting inferences according to experience and available evidence \cite{wang-2006-rigid-flexibility-the-logic-of-intelligence}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      Its strength lies in adaptive reasoning under uncertainty, but it lacks explicit neural and biological grounding and does not model self-awareness, language, homeostasis, and allostasis as an integrated mechanism for sustained autonomy.

      \subsection{BECCA}
      BECCA is a brain-emulating cognition and control architecture that combines online feature creation, internal-model learning, and reinforcement learning to support adaptation in previously unknown environments \cite{rohrer-2011-an-implemented-architecture-for-feature-creation-and-general-reinforcement-learning}.

      \paragraph{Deficiency}
      Although it emphasizes adaptive and brain-inspired control, it does not explicitly incorporate language-enhanced self-awareness or connect higher-order cognition to homeostatic and allostatic regulation for maintaining the agent over extended periods.
