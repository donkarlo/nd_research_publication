    This research proposes a framework for self awareness in Multi robot systems by suggesting the evolution of sensory action potential trains to a mutual language inspired by neurology and classical linguistics. The highest form of self-awareness is meant which is the awareness over robot's awareness in the sense that the system can use it for autonomous navigation and updating it's generative model. By language, Saussure’s syntagmatic and paradigmatic relations, and Lévi-Strauss’s theory of binary oppositions is meant. It first builds an internal language based on  event specific knowledge gained through guided learning. Then shares it's essential constituents with the other robots to detect self from other or novel sensory data from what already is experienced and memorized.
