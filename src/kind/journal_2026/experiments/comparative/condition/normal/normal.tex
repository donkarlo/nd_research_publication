   \section{LiDAR}
      Although the simulated RPLIDAR A2 2D LiDAR used in this research can scan up to 15 m ahead, ranges beyond 5 m are truncated to 5 m to avoid distortion caused when one quadcopter scans the other. Additionally, DBSCAN is applied to the points rather than to the original distances produced by the LiDAR.
      For the \(i\)-th LiDAR measurement, let \(r_i\) denote the measured range
      and \(\theta_i\) the corresponding beam angle. The endpoint of the beam
      in the LiDAR coordinate frame is

      \[
      \mathbf{p}_i
      =
      \begin{bmatrix}
         x_i\\
         y_i
      \end{bmatrix}
      =
      \begin{bmatrix}
         r_i\cos(\theta_i)\\
         r_i\sin(\theta_i)
      \end{bmatrix}.
      \]

      Hence, a LiDAR scan consisting of \(N\) range--angle measurements is
      converted to the point set

      \[
      \mathcal{P}
      =
      \left\{
      \mathbf{p}_i
      =
      \begin{bmatrix}
         r_i\cos(\theta_i)\\
         r_i\sin(\theta_i)
      \end{bmatrix}
      \;\middle|\;
      i=1,\ldots,N
      \right\}.
      \]
